# PATHFINDER

### AI-Powered Career Discovery & Personalised Growth Platform

This document describes the proposed features of PATHFINDER and what each feature does. It focuses on product functionality and explanations, rather than implementation instructions.

## 1. Home Page

- **Hero section** — Introduces PATHFINDER with a clear headline, short description, and prominent actions to begin career discovery or explore careers.
- **Nova mascot** — Introduces Nova, the friendly AI career companion, and provides a recognisable visual identity for the platform.
- **Find Your Career** — Starts the interactive career discovery quiz.
- **Explore Careers** — Takes visitors directly to the career catalogue so they can browse roles without completing the quiz first.
- **Career DNA preview** — Shows an example of how the platform can summarise a student's interests and preferences after assessment.
- **Career categories preview** — Highlights areas such as software development, AI, data, cybersecurity, cloud, and design.
- **How It Works** — Explains the journey from discovering interests to exploring careers, developing skills, and following a learning plan.
- **Sample recommendations** — Previews the type of career results users may receive. Any sample scores should be labelled illustrative.
- **Final call-to-action and footer** — Provides another entry point to the quiz plus useful navigation, about, contact, and privacy information.

---

## 2. Career Discovery Quiz

- **Interest and preference questions** — Collects information about topics and activities the student enjoys, such as programming, design, data, AI, or security.
- **Skills and experience questions** — Captures current skills and experience so the system can distinguish existing ability from interest and willingness to learn.
- **Work-style and career-priority questions** — Explores preferences such as analytical versus creative tasks, collaboration, research, and other work activities.
- **Question navigation and progress** — Shows progress, allows users to go back and change answers, and validates required responses.
- **Adaptive questions** — Optionally selects follow-up questions based on previous answers to explore a student's interests in more detail.
- **Nova's contextual reactions** — Provides relevant encouragement and explanations based on the student's answers rather than unrelated or random messages.
- **Save and resume** — Preserves quiz progress so users can continue later, subject to the platform's privacy choices.

---

## 3. Career Recommendation Engine

- **Ranked career matches** — Returns a shortlist of careers that align with the student's recorded interests, skills, and preferences.
- **Compatibility score** — Summarises how closely a career matches the profile under the system's scoring rules. It is not a probability of career success.
- **Recommendation explanations** — Shows which answers or profile features contributed to each recommendation.
- **Alternative careers** — Offers other plausible options so users are not led to believe there is only one suitable career.
- **Recommendation feedback** — Lets users indicate whether a recommendation interests them, providing feedback for future refinement.

---

## 4. Career Profile — Career DNA

- **Personal profile** — Stores the student's stated interests, skills, experience, preferences, and career goals.
- **Visual interest profile** — Uses a chart or visual indicators to make the student's selected interests and preferences easier to understand.
- **Editable profile** — Allows users to update their skills, interests, and goals as they learn more about themselves.
- **Saved careers and preferences** — Keeps selected careers and relevant choices available for later visits.
- **Profile refresh** — Allows users to retake the quiz and see how their recommendations change when their answers change.

---

## 5. Explore Careers

- **Career search** — Finds careers by name, keyword, or relevant terms.
- **Career categories** — Groups careers into areas such as software, AI/ML, data, cybersecurity, cloud, design, networking, mobile, and game development.
- **Filters** — Narrows results by category, interests, skills, education background, experience level, location, salary range, work style, or learning level when data is available.
- **Sorting** — Lets users organise results using supported criteria such as relevance or salary range.
- **Surprise Me** — Suggests a less familiar career related to the user's interests or to skills shared with careers they already like.

---

## 6. Career Details Page

- **Career overview** — Explains what the role is and the kinds of problems or tasks it typically involves.
- **Typical responsibilities** — Describes common work activities and what a day in the role may look like.
- **Skills and education** — Lists commonly used skills, relevant subjects, and possible entry routes.
- **Salary and demand information** — Shows location- and experience-specific salary or labour-market information when reliable sources are available, with source and update date.
- **Projects and learning resources** — Suggests beginner projects and verified learning resources that help users explore the role.
- **Related careers** — Shows adjacent roles and possible alternative paths.
- **Career actions** — Allows users to save a career, compare it with another, add it to a roadmap, or ask Nova about it.

---

## 7. Career Comparison

- **Side-by-side comparison** — Lets users compare two or more careers using consistent categories.
- **Skills and responsibilities comparison** — Highlights shared skills, important differences, typical work activities, and learning requirements.
- **Entry-path comparison** — Shows relevant foundations, beginner projects, and preparation options for each career.
- **Decision support** — Helps users explore trade-offs without declaring one career universally better than another.

---

## 8. Skill-Gap Analysis

- **Current-skill overview** — Summarises the skills a student reports having and their self-assessed level.
- **Career requirements comparison** — Compares the profile with a structured list of skills commonly used in the selected career.
- **Skills to develop** — Highlights potential learning priorities based on the difference between current and target skills.
- **Learning actions** — Connects each priority to resources, practice activities, or projects.
- **Progress updates** — Allows the student to revise skill levels as they practise and gain experience.

---

## 9. Personalised Learning Roadmap

- **Career-specific stages** — Organises learning into a sequence of foundations, skill development, projects, and portfolio preparation.
- **Prerequisites and milestones** — Shows which concepts or activities should be completed before moving to later stages.
- **Resources and projects** — Connects roadmap steps to suitable courses, references, and practical projects.
- **Progress tracking** — Lets users mark activities complete and view progress through the roadmap.
- **Flexible pathways** — Allows students to explore alternative sequences based on their existing knowledge, available time, and goals.

---

## 10. Nova — AI Career Mentor

- **Career Q&A** — Answers questions about career roles, skills, preparation, and learning paths.
- **Profile-aware guidance** — Uses relevant quiz answers, selected careers, and roadmap context to make responses more personal.
- **Recommendation explanations** — Explains the reasoning behind the platform's career matches using the recommendation engine's supporting information.
- **Project and learning suggestions** — Suggests next steps connected to a student's selected career and current skill level.
- **Suggested prompts** — Offers example questions to help users begin a conversation.
- **Demo versus live AI** — Clearly distinguishes predefined demo responses from responses generated by a connected language model.

---

## 11. Career Tryout Lab

- **Short career activities** — Offers small, introductory tasks inspired by different careers, such as a simple data analysis, programming exercise, design critique, or simulated security scenario.
- **Reflection and feedback** — Asks users whether they enjoyed an activity and how challenging it felt.
- **Related exploration** — Uses expressed interest in activities to suggest areas the student may want to explore further, without treating task performance as a definitive aptitude test.

---

## 12. Course and Project Explorer

- **Learning resource search** — Helps users find courses, tutorials, documentation, and other learning resources.
- **Resource filters** — Filters resources by subject, difficulty, prerequisites, or estimated duration where that information is known.
- **Project recommendations** — Suggests practical projects that align with the user's career interests and current skills.
- **Bookmarks and completion** — Lets users save resources and record completed learning activities.
- **Verified links** — Uses checked resource links and avoids inventing external course URLs.

---

## 13. Student Dashboard

- **Personal overview** — Brings together career recommendations, saved careers, and the user's current goals.
- **Learning progress** — Shows roadmap milestones and saved or completed learning activities.
- **Next-step suggestions** — Highlights a useful next action, such as exploring a career, practising a skill, or continuing a roadmap.
- **Quick access** — Provides convenient links to the career profile, explorer, roadmap, quiz, and Nova.

---

## 14. Advanced ML and Evaluation

- **Content-based recommendation** — Matches student profiles to structured career profiles using attributes such as interests, skills, and preferences.
- **Similarity scoring** — Uses methods such as cosine similarity to compare feature representations when appropriate.
- **Supervised career model** — Optionally trains a classifier if a suitable labelled dataset is available and the prediction target is clearly defined.
- **Semantic career search** — Uses text embeddings to find careers relevant to natural-language queries, even when the wording differs from career titles.
- **Clustering exploration** — Investigates patterns among student preference profiles. Any clusters must be validated and should not be presented as proven personality types.
- **Evaluation and fairness** — Evaluates recommendations with suitable ranking or classification metrics, compares models with a baseline, and checks for limitations or bias.

---

## 15. Privacy, Accessibility, and Reliability

- **Data controls** — Explains what profile information is stored and gives users appropriate control over saved information.
- **Accessible interaction** — Supports keyboard navigation, readable contrast, semantic controls, and clear focus indicators.
- **Responsive design** — Keeps the experience usable across desktop, laptop, tablet, and mobile screens.
- **Feedback and error states** — Provides clear loading, empty, error, and success messages so users understand what is happening.
- **Honest presentation** — Labels demo features, sample scores, and estimated career information accurately; avoids presenting recommendations as guaranteed outcomes.

## Suggested User Journey

> **Discover Yourself → Receive Career Matches → Explore Career Details → Compare Options → Identify Skills to Develop → Follow a Learning Roadmap → Get Guidance from Nova**

> **Note:** The features are proposed capabilities. Some, especially live AI responses, salary and demand data, adaptive assessments, and trained ML models, require suitable data sources or additional implementation before they can be described as live functionality.
